# Implementation decisions and gaps

The pre-build spec is preserved unchanged. For the timeboxed local prototype, conflict reporting identifies conflicting invoice IDs and counts, while competing full records remain in the unmodified CSV rather than being copied to a separate reject file. The first occurrence is always selected; duplicate counts compare the original fields, rather than normalized fields. There is no separate persisted clean or quarantine file: the in-memory database contains canonical rows with NULLs for invalid dependent fields, and stdout reports aggregate quality. These choices reduce scope and do not constitute a full row-level rejection/lineage report. The original source remains available for investigation.

The prototype validates fields required by its metrics but does not fully validate signup dates, billing-cycle-dependent invoice pricing, support tickets or every noncritical field. MRR does not depend on billing cycle. Region values are normalized but not restricted to a whitelist. Rounding is to USD cents per invoice (half-up) and to cents per account MRR. Exclusion counts are aggregate rather than broken down per result. Zero-value refunds are excluded alongside positive ones. A router recognizes fixed keyword combinations rather than interpreting arbitrary requests; users should use the six documented questions.
